huedrop – Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use huedrop.

Who we are

huedrop is a wallpaper app made by Anwesh Mishra (huedropstudio). You can reach us at huedrop.studio@gmail.com.

No account, no sign-up

You don’t create an account, and huedrop doesn’t ask for your name, email, phone number or exact location.

What stays on your phone

Your likes, favourites, download list and the photos or videos you choose are stored only on your device. huedrop doesn’t upload them to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Photos and videos you pick

When you add your own photos or videos, Android’s picker lets you choose each item. Effects such as depth are worked out on your phone. Your media isn’t sent to our servers.

Ads (Google AdMob)

huedrop shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep its service working. In the EEA, the UK and some other regions Google asks for your ad choices first. More: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. huedrop only learns, on your device, whether your subscription is active.

Other network use

Some features download small files, for example a font, the first time you use them. Whoever hosts the file can see your IP address, like any website.

Device sensors

Motion sensors are used on your phone only, to make wallpapers tilt and move. Readings aren’t stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

We ask for permissions only when a feature needs them, and explain why first. See Permissions in Privacy & legal.

Your choices

• Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
• Use Privacy options in Privacy & legal (where available) to change your ad choices.
• Use Withdraw consent in Privacy & legal to be asked again.
• To ask about your information, write to huedrop.studio@gmail.com.

Children

huedrop is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Keeping data safe

Ads and downloads use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Your own content stays on your phone, protected by your phone’s security.

Changes

If this policy changes we’ll update the date below, and ask for your agreement again when the change matters.
